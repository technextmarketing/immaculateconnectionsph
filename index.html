Good to know

Questions travellers ask before booking

How do I book a tour package?
Send an inquiry through the form, Facebook Messenger or by phone with your preferred package, dates and number of people. We confirm availability with our partners and send you a quotation with the full inclusions. Payment details and booking confirmation follow once you approve the quotation.
Can a package be customised?
Yes. Every itinerary can be tailored to your preferences and interests: add a night, combine tours, change the pick-up point or build a trip from scratch. Choose “Custom itinerary” on the inquiry form and describe what you have in mind.
What do the packages include?
Each package lists its inclusions, for example two-way boat tickets, terminal and entrance fees, a licensed tour guide, air-conditioned transportation and meals. Open any package for the full list and the places you will visit.
Do you arrange transport and hotels separately from tours?
Yes. Vans, coasters, buses and 4-seaters can be reserved on their own, and hotel or resort stays can be booked as a custom reservation. Rates vary by location and dates and are quoted on request.
Do you handle company outings, seminars and government events?
We do. Our MICE service covers meetings, incentives, conferences, exhibitions, trainings and seminars, including orientation courses for local government units. Tell us the type of event, number of participants and dates and we will prepare a proposal.
