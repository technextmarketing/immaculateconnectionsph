Good to know

Questions travellers ask before booking

How do I book a tour package?
Open the package page and click “Request a quote” or “Book this offer”, or use the inquiry form and choose your package. We confirm availability for your dates and send a quotation with the payment details. Your booking is confirmed once payment is received.
Are the special-offer prices per person?
Yes. The Da Nang and Hanoi–Sapa–Ha Long Bay rates are per pax based on twin sharing, and peak dates carry the surcharge shown beside each date. Exclusions such as visa, travel tax, tips and single supplement are listed on every package page.
Can you customise a package?
Yes. Our tours are tailored to your preferences and interests. Choose “Custom itinerary” in the inquiry form and describe your dates, group size and the places you want to see.
Where do local tours depart from?
Cebu tours pick up from Cebu City or Mactan hotels. Bohol and Camotes packages include two-way boat tickets from Cebu. Exact meeting points and times are confirmed in your booking voucher.
Do you handle groups, seminars and company events?
Yes. From seminars and orientation courses to corporate gatherings and reunions, we arrange venues, hotel blocks, coaster and bus transport and meals. Use the inquiry form and choose “Event or seminar”.
