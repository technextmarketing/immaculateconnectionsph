Service 04

Local & International Tour Packages

Expertly crafted itineraries across Cebu, Bohol and the Camotes Islands, island getaways to Siargao, Boracay, El Nido and Coron, and international tours to Vietnam, China and Japan. Every package lists its inclusions and the places you will visit.

  • Cebu day tours: Queen City Tour, Twin City Tour and the Cebu Highlands Vista Tour.
  • Bohol & Camotes: Bohol Breezes with Loboc River lunch, Discover Enchanting Camotes and Lakwatsa sa Tudela (2 days 1 night).
  • Island getaways: Siargao 3D2N, with Boracay, El Nido and Coron coming soon.
  • International: Da Nang, Hanoi–Sapa–Ha Long Bay, Shanghai, Yunnan and Japan programmes with fixed 2026 departures and published rates.
